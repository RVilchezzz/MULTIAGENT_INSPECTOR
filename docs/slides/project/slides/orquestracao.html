Design lessons: start with a simple fixed workflow (Anthropic, 2024); multi-agent gains are often small, so every agent must earn its place in an ablation (MAST, NeurIPS 2025).

The attached paper (Adimulam et al.) is a position paper: it gives the vocabulary, not experiments. We take three ideas from it: the roles (worker, service, support agents), an orchestration layer that decides who does what and in which order, and validating every output against a schema. On the right is how our five components map onto those roles. Two lessons from the literature keep the design honest: Anthropic recommends starting with a simple fixed workflow, and MAST (a study of why multi-agent systems fail) shows gains over a single agent or best-of-N are often minimal, so each agent will be removed in an ablation to prove it helps.
