Real MMAD pair. Map: our illustrative 1-shot patch comparison with DINOv2 ViT-S/14 on this pair (the project will use DINOv3). True answer: bottom right.

Walk through one real image. Input: the good bottle and the broken one, plus the question. Step 1, the visual expert (frozen DINOv3) splits both photos into small patches and, for each patch of the part, measures how far it is from the most similar patch of the reference. The heat map you see was computed by us on this exact pair with DINOv2, and it lights up the broken rim. The peak is turned into text, a cell of a 3×3 grid such as "bottom right, high score", and a zoomed crop. (On this pair our quick DINOv2 version put the peak in the bottom-center cell, next to the true bottom-right answer: calibrating this is part of Delivery II.) Step 2, two readers from different model families answer in JSON. Step 3, the verifier checks the format, consistency and agreement with the expert, and reopens at most once to avoid loops. Step 4, the aggregator votes with weights fixed on the dev split. Step 5, everything is logged.
