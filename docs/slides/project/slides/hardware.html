We do have hardware. Locally, a 6 GB laptop GPU is enough for the DINOv3 expert and for debugging the full pipeline with a small 4B model in 4-bit. The two 8B readers run on Colab Pro with vLLM, a fast batched inference server, on L4 or T4 GPUs; we avoid the A100 because it burns compute units 4 to 5 times faster. Each agent runs over the whole split, saves its answers to Drive and frees the GPU, so two 8B models are never loaded together and a disconnect does not lose work. Charles has no local GPU and works directly on Colab.
