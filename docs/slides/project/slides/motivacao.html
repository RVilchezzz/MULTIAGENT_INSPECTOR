This is why the core agent is a visual expert. In MMAD, when the MLLM is told where the defect is (ground truth turned into text), discrimination jumps by about 25 points and localization by 28. Off-the-shelf anomaly detectors (PatchCore, AnomalyCLIP) are not precise enough and even hurt discrimination, because they also fire on normal images. SiGMA shows a multi-agent team with a DINOv3 expert reaches 85.2%, but with up to 256 normal images, retrieved domain text, a team chosen on MMAD answers and 4 GPUs. Our question: how much of that gain survives with one reference photo and no tuning on the test?
