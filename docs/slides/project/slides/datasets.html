Use: evaluation only, no training. github.com/jam-cc/MMAD · huggingface.co/datasets/jiang-cc/MMAD (corrected revision, Oct 22, 2026).

MMAD is a public benchmark (ICLR 2025) built on four industrial inspection datasets with 38 product categories. The figure shows its composition: normal and abnormal images in the center, the 7 subtasks in the middle ring and the categories outside. It comes fully labeled, so we annotate nothing; labels are only used to score answers. MMAD is test-only, so we make our own split: 10% for development, where we are allowed to look at answers while choosing prompts and weights, and a test sample from the rest, run once after the configuration is frozen on Nov 19. Limitation: one view per part; Real-IAD (5 views) is an optional extension.
